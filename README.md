# Tejas

A browser Game Boy–style game for **Grade 7 Texas History (TEKS §113.19)**.

**Play it: https://acespartiate.github.io/tejas/**

Session one is **Goliad** — the town and Presidio La Bahía — **2 to 9 October
1835**, the week after Gonzales. That week is load-bearing: the fight over the
cannon has already happened somewhere else, so it reaches the player as a story
a rider brought.

## Playing

Nothing to install. Open the link on a laptop, a Chromebook, or a phone.

- **Move** — arrow keys, WASD, a gamepad, or the on-screen pad
- **Talk, read, pick up** — `F`, or `A`
- **Step back, close** — `G`, or `B`

On a phone the game puts itself in a handheld case: hold it upright for the
tall body, turn it sideways for the wide one.

## What it is

Everything a player finds is filed as a **Record** — a page, a tally, a title,
a survey. Records are artifacts, never people: the person who made it is cited
on it, and what you hold is the paper. In an argument they beat each other in a
triangle: **Word beats Law, Law beats Count, Count beats Word.**

Where a story is folklore, the game says so and makes that the lesson. Places
are drawn from the *Handbook of Texas* rather than invented.

## About this repository

This holds the built game and nothing else — one self-contained HTML file with
